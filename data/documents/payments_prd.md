# Payment Processing — Product Requirements Document

Document ID: PRD-205
Owner: Payments Product Team
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Background

NovaTech supports credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets for online purchases.

Payment failures are a major source of unsuccessful transactions.

## Payment Performance

Product analytics show that 94% of payment attempts are successfully completed.

The most common causes of payment failure are insufficient funds, expired cards, and incorrect payment information.

## Objective

Increase payment success while maintaining fraud prevention and transaction security.

## Primary Metric

Payment success rate.
