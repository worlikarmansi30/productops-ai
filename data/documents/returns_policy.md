# Returns Policy

Document ID: POL-201
Owner: Customer Experience Team
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Background

NovaTech customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the customer's original payment method.

Refund processing typically takes 5–7 business days after the returned product is received.

## Exceptions

Final-sale products and personalized products are not eligible for returns.
